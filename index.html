Sargasso

Founder / creator · Active development

Sargasso is an AI-powered sargassum monitoring and forecasting project being developed in St. Kitts and Nevis. The current system combines satellite imagery with environmental and oceanographic data to estimate present-day conditions and produce a 48-hour coastal forecast.

  • Satellite-based sargassum detection and segmentation.
  • 79,527 training samples and 17,149 validation samples processed.
  • Operational end-to-end forecasting pipeline.
  • 48-hour forecasts across 18 coastal stretches around St. Kitts and Nevis.
  • Environmental and oceanographic inputs integrated into the model stack.
  • Next: deeper validation and research, low-cost coastal sensing hardware, then autonomous sensing and cleanup systems.
  • Next: funding pls.
