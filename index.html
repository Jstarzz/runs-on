SARGASSO

Role: Founder / Creator  |  Status: Active development  |  Flagship project

Sargasso is an AI-powered sargassum monitoring and forecasting project being developed in St. Kitts and Nevis. It combines satellite-based detection with environmental and oceanographic data to track sargassum conditions and predict where it will accumulate next.

Current System

  • Satellite-based sargassum detection and segmentation.
  • Roughly 79,500 training samples and 17,100 validation samples processed.
  • End-to-end forecasting pipeline is operational.
  • Produces present-day sargassum conditions and a 48-hour forecast.
  • Forecast output currently covers coastal stretches around St. Kitts and Nevis.
  • Combines machine learning with environmental/oceanographic data.

Where This Is Going

Sargasso is not just a machine learning experiment. It's being built as an evolving environmental technology platform spanning forecasting, sensing, research, and eventually hardware and robotics:

  • Research papers and further model validation are planned.
  • Inexpensive physical sensing hardware for coastal monitoring.
  • Coastal monitoring devices deployed near affected shorelines.
  • Eventually, automated detection and cleanup systems.
